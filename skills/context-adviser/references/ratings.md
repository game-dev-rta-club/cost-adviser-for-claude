# Rating an option

Rate every proposal A, B or C on each axis below, in its `ratings`
(`ease`, `safety`, `savings`). **Do not propose an option rated C on any
axis.** If nothing good is left for a finding, leave it out instead of offering
weak options. The report shows the ratings as stars: two for B on every axis,
one more for each A (A on all three is five stars).

| Axis | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ease of use (`ease`) | Set up once, then it keeps working with no attention | A small routine at natural points, or a setup that needs occasional upkeep | The user must remember it every time |
| Few side effects (`safety`) | No loss of quality, speed or context expected | A small, visible cost that is easy to undo | Risks the quality of the work or adds real friction |
| Savings (`savings`) | Touches 20% or more of cost, as the numbers show | Touches 5–20% of cost | Under 5%, or no clear link to the numbers |

Base `savings` on the share of cost the option touches in the linked view, and
say that share in the detail. Rate from these definitions, not from a general
impression, so the same option gets the same rating each time.
